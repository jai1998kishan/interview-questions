Absolutely. If you're preparing for a MERN Stack interview with 0–1 year of experience, don't try to learn everything in the MERN ecosystem. Focus on the fundamentals interviewers repeatedly test, then build enough practical knowledge to explain your projects confidently.

Phase 1 — JavaScript Fundamentals
Spend ~2 weeks here.

This is probably the most important part of your preparation.

A common mistake is:

"I'm preparing for MERN, so I'll directly start React."

Don't do that.

React, Node and Express all become much easier when your JavaScript is strong.
